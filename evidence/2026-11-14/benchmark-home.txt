wojak-bench gives an ai model a wojak drawing and asks for a figure you can hold. you pick the
drawing and the model. nobody sees the result before it is printed, including you. it is printed
in white pla on the office printer at andon labs in san francisco, and every result is added to
the list below, good or bad. order a blind figure, $29
